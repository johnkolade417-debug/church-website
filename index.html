WHO WE ARE

ABOUT FIREBRAND MODEL PARISH

Firebrand Model Parish is a vibrant Christian community devoted to spiritual growth, unity, and joyful service. We are building a welcoming environment where people can grow in faith, form meaningful relationships, and encounter God in a real way.

Our mission is to ignite the fire of God's love in every heart and empower people to live out their faith with boldness, compassion, and purpose.

  • Christ-centered worship that draws people closer to God
  • A warm community where lives are nurtured and strengthened
  • Practical teaching that inspires faith-filled living every day
